# GRADI FESTIVE EXPERIENCE — Lead Magnets & Nurture

The goal of a lead magnet is not "free stuff". It has to be something **only a future customer
would want**, and that gets us a phone number or email we can reach again.

## LM1 — La Liste Privée (B2C, main magnet)
**Promise:** « Soyez les premiers. »
- 🥂 Cocktail de bienvenue offert on your first visit (until 31 Dec, 1 per person)
- 🗓️ 24h priority access to bookings before the public (opening weekend + December Saturdays)
- 🎟️ Draw: 20 duos invited to the **Preview Night** (share + tag to enter)
- 🔢 A personal list number (« Vous êtes le n° 512 »). Being early feels like status.

**Why it works:** scarcity (priority access), status (number, Preview Night), low cost to us
(cocktail cost ~CHF 3, and it drives a visit that averages CHF 90+/person).

**Capture:** IG DM flow A, Meta Instant Form C2, `landing.html` form.
**Fields:** first name, phone (WhatsApp), email (optional), party type, opt-in.

## LM2 — Kit Fêtes d'Entreprise 2026 (B2B, highest value)
A 6-page PDF, designed in the brand style:
1. Cover: « Votre fête de fin d'année, clé en main. »
2. 3 formulas: *Apéro Festif* (standing, CHF [X]/pers), *Dîner de Fêtes* (seated, CHF [Y]),
   *Soirée Privatisée* (exclusive venue, from CHF [Z])
3. Capacities & floor plan (seated / standing / private area)
4. Live calendar: dates still free in December (update weekly; scarcity is real)
5. Extras: DJ, photo booth, branded menu cards, gift for each guest, invoice to the company
6. « Réservez avant le 15 novembre » bonus: **free welcome champagne glass for the whole
   group**, plus the contact for Valbon (direct WhatsApp)

**Capture:** IG DM flow B, Meta C4, LinkedIn Lead Gen, `landing.html` B2B form, outreach emails.
**SLA:** call back within 24h (2h if 50+ guests). Quote sent same day.

## LM3 — Pré-vente Nouvel An (B2C, December)
- « Me prévenir » list → early-bird **-20% for 48h** on 1 Dec
- People who join get access 1h before everyone else
- Capture: DM flow C, C6 ads, landing

## Data & compliance (Switzerland nLPD / EU RGPD)
- Explicit, unticked opt-in checkbox for WhatsApp/email marketing, with a link to the privacy notice
- Store: source (`ig_dm`, `meta_form`, `landing`, `linkedin`, `outreach`), date, consent timestamp
- Every WhatsApp/email has a way out (« Répondez STOP »)
- Single source of truth: one Google Sheet / CRM fed by ManyChat + forms via Zapier
