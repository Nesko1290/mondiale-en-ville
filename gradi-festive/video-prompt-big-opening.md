# GRADI — Big Opening video prompt (10 s, 9:16)

Prompt to paste into the video model (kept in English, models follow it better). On-screen
French titles are added in editing, see the plan at the bottom.

```
ROLE: You are the art director for GRADI Festif Expérience, a new late-night supper club in
Geneva opening its doors. Create a 10-second vertical video (9:16, 1080x1920) announcing our
BIG OPENING WEEK on 5, 6 and 7 November. Everything must feel on-brand for GRADI; do not copy
any other restaurant or venue.

BRAND BRIEF:
- Name: GRADI Festif Expérience, "the art of dining, dancing and living the moment"
- Concept: an old-world supper club. Dinner turns into music, music turns into dancing.
- Hero dish: fresh tagliatelle with black truffle shaved at the table, served in a deep
  midnight-blue ceramic bowl
- Hero drink: champagne in vintage coupe glasses
- Brand colours: deep bordeaux #631C21 (primary) and forest green #1F3627 (secondary), with
  warm cream #EFE2CC. Use them in the set: bordeaux velvet curtains and armchairs, dark green
  leather Chesterfield banquettes, bordeaux linen napkins, cream tablecloths
- Mood: festive, intimate, seductive, generous; the place everyone wants to be at first, at night
- Language of on-screen text: French (added in editing, never generated)

SET & LIGHT:
Small round tables with heavy white tablecloths, each lit by a brass table lamp with a pleated
cream lampshade. Dark green tufted leather banquettes, bordeaux velvet armchairs and heavy
velvet curtains, Persian rugs in red, cream and green on the floor, dark wood panelling, deep
red roses on the tables. All light comes from the table lamps and a few wall sconces: low,
warm, golden pools of light with deep shadows in between. No string lights, no balloons, no
Christmas decorations, no gold glitter, no neon.

FORMAT (one continuous handheld shot, no cuts, cinematic):
0-1s   Inside GRADI at night. The camera faces a bordeaux velvet curtain that parts slightly as a
       smiling host walks through it toward the lens: elegant, dressed in a black waistcoat
       over a crisp white shirt with a plain bordeaux apron (no logo, no embroidery), holding a
       folded bordeaux linen napkin.
1-3s   She lifts the napkin toward the lens as if wiping the camera clean, playful, with a
       knowing smile, then lowers it.
3-6s   She reaches toward the lens and makes a slow "pinch-to-zoom" gesture with her fingers, as
       if grabbing and enlarging something floating in front of her face, then gives a small,
       confident nod (not a thumbs-up) and steps aside out of frame.
6-9s   The camera glides past her into the room: lamplit tables full of elegantly dressed guests,
       two friends clinking champagne coupes, a hand shaving black truffle over the tagliatelle,
       a woman in a dark green dress laughing, red patent heels under a table. In the
       background the lights begin to dim and a few couples start dancing.
9-10s  The camera settles on a wide view of the room: tables glowing under their lampshades in
       the foreground, people dancing softly in the back. Hold still.

STYLE: Shot on a modern cinema camera with vintage anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field,
warm tungsten grade with deep bordeaux reds and dark green shadows, cream highlights, natural
35mm film grain, realistic skin tones, no flash, no daylight. It should feel like a scene from a
film set in a timeless Parisian or Viennese supper club. Real, joyful, slightly candid: not a
stiff commercial.

IMPORTANT: Generate NO text, letters, numbers, logos, signs or captions anywhere in the video,
including on menus, aprons, walls and glasses. Keep the area just above the host's face
(upper-middle of the frame) visually clean and fairly dark: animated titles will be added there
in editing and must line up with her pinch-to-zoom gesture at 3-6s.
```

Music: warm, slow-burning disco or nu-jazz groove that builds at 6 s, when the room appears.

## What changed from the original prompt, and why
- **Interior:** string lights, balloons and gold are gone (forbidden by the brand guide). They're
  replaced by the moodboard's own codes: lamplight, velvet, leather banquettes, Persian rugs.
- **Hero dish:** truffle tagliatelle, as in the moodboard. No wood-fired pizza oven.
- **Colours:** bordeaux, forest and cream are set in the props (curtains, banquettes,
  napkins, tablecloths) rather than in coloured lights.
- **Host:** a waistcoat and a plain apron with no logo, because the model must not generate any
  text or logo. A nod instead of a thumbs-up, which fits the brand's tone better.
- **Ending:** the lights dim and people dance, the third brand pillar (*music · food · drinks*).
- **Opening:** she comes through the velvet curtain, the same image as the Instagram visuals and
  the reveal video.
