# GRADI Festif Expérience — Brand Guide (from the moodboard)

**What GRADI is:** a late-night supper club. Dinner turns into music, music turns into dancing.
Old-world glamour: velvet banquettes, lampshade table lamps, white tablecloths, Persian rugs,
champagne coupes. It's festive because the *night* is the party, not because of Christmas
decorations. Every creative must feel like the moodboard: dark, warm, intimate, a little
mysterious.

## Signatures (use exactly as written)
| Line | Use |
|------|-----|
| **GRADI** / *Festif Expérience* | Logo lockup: bold sans + spaced small caps underneath |
| *The art of dining, dancing and living the moment.* | Brand promise (FR: *L'art de dîner, de danser et de vivre l'instant.*) |
| *Eat late, stay later.* | Hero line for ads, reels end-cards, stories |
| *music · food · drinks* | Handwritten, the three pillars |

The English signatures stay in English (Geneva is international and they're part of the
identity). Everything around them (offers, CTAs, DMs) is in French.

## Type
- **Logo / UI:** bold geometric sans (web stand-in: *Outfit 800*; use the real logo file in creatives)
- **Statements:** soft high-contrast serif (web stand-in: *Gloock*)
- **Handwritten:** chalky script for short lines only (web stand-in: *Caveat*)

## Emblem
The **moon with a face and a four-point star**: night, mystery, late hours. Use it as the
profile picture, story highlight covers, the reveal reel, menu covers, the wax seal on
invitations, and the review-QR card.

## Photo direction
- Low, warm light from table lamps. Deep shadows, no flash, no daylight
- Close details: hands, a coupe on velvet, truffle being shaved, the menu covering a face
- People seen from behind or cropped. Guests are characters, not models posing at the camera
- Textures: velvet, leather, the rug, white linen
- **Avoid:** stock "Christmas dinner", fairy-light clichés, bright food-delivery style shots

## Tone of voice
Confident, seductive, short. We **invite**, we don't sell.
- ✅ « Une table vous attend. La nuit aussi. »
- ✅ « On dîne tard. On reste plus tard encore. »
- ❌ « PROMO ! Réservez vite !!! 🎄🎅 »
Emoji: rarely, and only ✦ 🌙 🥂.
