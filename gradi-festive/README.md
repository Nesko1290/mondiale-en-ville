## Positioning

**Gradi Festive Experience: plus qu'un dîner, une soirée.**
Not "a new restaurant". It's *the* place to celebrate the holidays in Geneva: a festive set,
live moments (fire, music, a midnight show), menus made for sharing and for groups.

Three audiences, three entry doors:

| Audience | What they want | Entry offer (lead magnet) | Money |
|----------|----------------|---------------------------|-------|
| **Couples & friends 25-45** | Be the first in, get a story for Instagram | *Liste Privée*: invitation to the Preview Night + welcome cocktail | Tables, weekends |
| **Corporate / HR / office managers** | A turnkey Christmas party without the hassle | *Kit Fêtes d'Entreprise 2026* (PDF: menus, prices, capacity, dates) | **Biggest ticket**: 20-120 pax groups |
| **Party crowd** | The NYE night everyone posts about | *Pré-vente Nouvel An*: early-bird price, 48h only | Fixed-price NYE seats |

> December corporate parties are the money in Geneva. Most companies book in Oct–Nov, so the
> B2B track starts **now** (J-28), before the B2C hype.
