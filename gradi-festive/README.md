## Positioning

**Gradi Festive Experience: plus qu'un dîner, une soirée.**
Not "a new restaurant". It's *the* place to celebrate the holidays in Geneva: a festive set,
live moments (fire, music, a midnight show), menus made for sharing and for groups.

Three audiences, three entry doors:

| Audience | What they want | Entry offer (lead magnet) | Money |
|----------|----------------|---------------------------|-------|
| **Couples & friends 25-45** | Be the first in, get a story for Instagram | *Liste Privée*: priority booking for opening week + welcome cocktail | Tables, weekends |
| **Corporate / HR / office managers** | A turnkey Christmas party without the hassle | *Kit Fêtes d'Entreprise 2026* (PDF) + **invitation to taste before booking** | **Biggest ticket**: 20-120 pax groups |
| **Party crowd** | The NYE night everyone posts about | *Pré-vente Nouvel An*: early-bird price, 48h only | Fixed-price NYE seats |

> **Opening on 5 Nov is an advantage for B2B.** Most Geneva companies are choosing their
> December venue right now, and we're one of the few places they can *come and taste* before
> they sign. Every office manager we bring in during November is a potential 50-person party.

## The 6-day sprint (J0 = Thu 5 Nov)

Six days is not enough to build an audience from zero, so the plan doesn't try. We **borrow
audiences** (creators, press, the team's own networks, existing followers) and use paid ads to
turn that attention into names on the list.
