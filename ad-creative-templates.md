## Format 5 — "Note qui s'écrit" Reel (1080×1920, video, 8-10s)

**Reference:** @monday.supperclub, Burger Night 3.0 (instagram.com/p/DewGmr4IJO6, Oct 2026, ~4.1k likes).
We copy the *format*, not their line or visuals.

**How it works**
- **Background:** ~15 close-up, flash-lit photos (food, hands, drinks, staff, branded menus/props),
  cut very fast (~8-10 cuts/second) and looped 3-4 times over the 9 seconds.
- **Overlay:** one blank paper note, fixed dead-centre for the whole video, never moves.
- **Text:** one sentence handwritten in red marker on the note, revealed word by word:
  - 0:00-0:01 — blank note (hook: "what's it going to say?")
  - 0:01 → 0:07 — one new word/chunk roughly every 1-1.5s
  - 0:07-0:09 — full line held to the end
- **Audio:** an upbeat track, cuts on the beat.
- **Caption does the selling:** event + price + what's included + scarcity ("places limitées").

**Shot list (15 shots, all flash, top-down or tight)**
Signature dish ×3 angles · hands grabbing food · sauce/cheese being added · empty plate with crumbs ·
drinks being poured · staff in apron · menu/print with the logo · branded mug/glass/napkin ·
table of friends laughing (faces optional) · tray/stack of the hero product · venue detail (sign, neon, cutlery pot).

**Line structure:** `[STOP DOING X] & [COME TO Y]` — 6-8 words max, split into 4-5 reveals.

**Mondiale variants (French, handwritten red #c8102e on bone #faf9f6 note)**
1. *Lâche ton canapé / & viens vivre / le Mondial / en ville*
2. *Coupe la télé / & viens / à la fanzone*
3. *Range ton téléphone / & regarde le match / avec nous*

**Caption template**
> Ce [jour] : [nom de l'événement] 🍔⚽
> [Prix] · [ce qui est inclus]
> Places limitées pour [heure]. Réservez → lien en bio

**AI generation:** see the kie.ai prompt in the next section. Generate the photos/background with AI,
then add the note + handwritten words in CapCut (AI video models garble text that changes word by word).

### kie.ai prompt — restaurant brand version

Fill in the BRAND BRIEF block, then paste the whole thing into kie.ai
(image model for the 15 stills, e.g. Nano Banana / Flux / GPT-4o Image; or a video model such as
Veo 3 / Kling for the background montage).

```text
ROLE: You are the art director for a new restaurant brand. Adapt the creative format below to THIS brand.
Do not copy any other restaurant's name, slogan, logo, menu or props — everything must be on-brand for us.

BRAND BRIEF (use this for every decision):
- Restaurant name: [NAME]
- Cuisine / hero dish: [e.g. smash burgers, Neapolitan pizza, Lebanese mezze]
- City / vibe: [e.g. Geneva, warm, lively, a bit rebellious]
- Brand colours: [primary HEX], [accent HEX], [neutral HEX]
- Props / branding to show: [logo on menus, mugs, napkins, neon sign, staff aprons]
- Language of on-screen text: [French / English]
- Event or offer to promote: [e.g. "Burger Night", Monday 19:00, CHF 39, 3 courses + 1 drink]

FORMAT: Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) Instagram/TikTok Reel, 9 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: Candid, on-camera-flash food photography at night. Hard direct flash, high contrast,
saturated warm colours, slight film grain, shot top-down or very close. Feels like a friend took them
at a busy dinner — real, messy, appetising, not a polished studio shoot. Colour grading leans into
the brand colours.

GENERATE 15 SHOTS (one image each, same lighting and grade across all):
1. Hero dish, close-up, top-down on branded paper/tray
2. Hero dish, side angle, steam/melting/glossy details
3. Hand picking up the hero dish
4. A sauce, cheese or garnish being added by a chef's hands
5. Stack/tray of the hero product or its key ingredient
6. Empty plate with crumbs and a napkin (the "it was that good" shot)
7. Drink being poured, brand-coloured glass or bottle
8. Staff member in a branded apron holding a plate (face cropped out)
9. Printed menu with the restaurant name clearly visible
10. Branded mug / glass / napkin on the table
11. Friends' hands reaching across a full table (no clear faces)
12. A side dish in a steel bowl
13. Venue detail: sign, neon or wall art in brand colours
14. Cutlery pot / table setting with a branded detail
15. Wide-ish shot of a full, lively table from above

KEEP THE CENTRE THIRD OF EVERY FRAME VISUALLY SIMPLE: a paper note will be overlaid there in editing.
NO text, letters or logos generated inside the images except the restaurant name on the menu (shot 9)
and the sign (shot 13).

IF USING A VIDEO MODEL: make one 9-second montage of these shots, hard cuts every ~0.1-0.15s,
looping the set 3-4 times, energetic pacing, no camera shake, no on-screen text.

ON-SCREEN TEXT (added in editing, not generated): one blank off-white paper note fixed in the centre,
a single 6-8 word line in handwritten marker in the accent colour, revealed word by word:
blank (0-1s) → word 1 (1s) → +words (2s) → +words (3s) → +words (5s) → full line (7-9s).
Write 3 line options in the brand language using the structure "[Stop doing X] & [come to Y]",
tied to the event/offer in the brief.

ALSO WRITE: an Instagram caption (max 4 lines): event + day, price, what's included,
limited seats + time, call to action.
```
