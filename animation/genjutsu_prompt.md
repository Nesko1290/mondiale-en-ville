GLOBAL RULES (every frame, highest priority)
Photoreal live-action film, Jamaica, golden late-afternoon sun, saturated tropical colours, warm cinematic grade, light film grain, nostalgic old-days travel mood. One continuous take, no cuts, camera path and timing exactly as Video 1. There is exactly one bus and it never stops, never slows, never changes lane; it drives fast with wheels spinning and dust behind it. The sea is always on the same side of the road as in Video 1; palms, jungle and fruit stands are on the other side. Natural motion blur on anything passing close to the camera. Text appears only in three places and is spelled exactly: the cloud letters GENEVA ARE YOU READY?, the bus lettering FROM THE CARIBBEAN TO GENEVA, and the billboard. No other text, signs, logos, captions or watermark.

MOMENT 1 (0.0-1.9s)
High above the Caribbean coast, giant fluffy cloud letters spell GENEVA / ARE YOU / READY? in the sky. The camera flies straight at them and bursts through the cloud in a puff of white mist.

MOMENT 2 (1.9-4.0s)
The camera dives out of the sky toward a coastline of jungle, coastal road, white beach and turquoise sea. A small colourful bus races along the road far below and grows fast in frame.

MOMENT 3 (4.0-5.4s)
The camera levels out just above the green roof of the bus, skimming a roof rack piled high with old leather suitcases and trunks tied down with rope, then slides off and drops down the sea-side flank to window height.

MOMENT 4 (5.4-8.0s)
Fast side-tracking shot: the yellow, red and green bus fills the frame, FROM THE CARIBBEAN TO GENEVA readable on its green band, passengers visible through the open windows, while roadside fruit stands and palm trees whip past behind it in a blur of colour.

MOMENT 5 (8.0-10.6s)
The camera swoops in through an open side window near the front and glides backwards down the centre aisle. Joyful Caribbean passengers in vintage 1960s travel clothes sit on both sides holding old leather suitcases on their laps, laughing and dancing in their seats, warm sunlight streaming through the windows. The camera flies out through the open rear window.

MOMENT 6 (10.6-12.7s)
Outside behind the bus, the camera swings round over the beach side to an elderly Rastafarian man standing on the sandy roadside. He watches the bus roar away, throws his hands up, then turns to the camera wide-eyed and shouts in Jamaican patois: "Bomboclaaat! What is this?!"

MOMENT 7 (12.7-15.5s)
The camera whips forward and races up the road, flying over the roof of the bus, toward a giant highway billboard standing over the road. It pushes in until the billboard fills the frame. The billboard is dark with a white border and reads, top to bottom: the yellow Bubble logo, SAT 28 NOV, KULTURE BAR, GENEVA, DJ BLITZ & GOLD UP, 22H - 04H.
