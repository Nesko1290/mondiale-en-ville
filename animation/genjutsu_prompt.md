GLOBAL RULES (every frame, highest priority)
Photoreal live-action film, Jamaica, golden late-afternoon sun, saturated tropical colours, warm cinematic grade, light film grain. One continuous take, no cuts, camera path and timing exactly as Video 1. There is exactly one bus and it never stops, never slows, never changes lane; it drives fast along the road with wheels spinning and a little dust behind it. The sea is always on the same side of the road as in Video 1, palms, jungle and fruit stands on the other side. Natural motion blur on anything passing close to the camera. No text, no logos, no captions, no watermark anywhere in the image.

MOMENT 1 (0.0–2.5s)
The camera falls out of a bright blue sky, punching through white clouds, diving toward a coastline: jungle, a coastal road, a white beach and turquoise sea. A small colourful bus races along the road far below and grows fast in frame.

MOMENT 2 (2.5–5.0s)
The camera levels out just above the green roof of the bus, flying with it down the road, then slides off the roof and drops down the sea-side flank of the bus to window height.

MOMENT 3 (5.0–7.8s)
Fast side-tracking shot from the sea side: the yellow, red and green bus fills the frame, and behind it roadside fruit stands and palm trees whip past in a blur of colour at high speed.

MOMENT 4 (7.8–9.6s)
The camera lets the bus go and settles on a Rastafarian man standing on the sandy roadside. As the bus blasts past him he flinches, throws his hands up, eyes wide, turns his head to follow it and shouts in Jamaican patois, shocked: "Bomboclaaat! What is this?!" The bus keeps racing away down the road behind him.

MOMENT 5 (9.6–12.0s)
The camera swings 180 degrees away from the road, out over the beach and the sea, and climbs fast into the sky, ending on a clean, empty expanse of warm blue sky with a few soft clouds, still rising.
