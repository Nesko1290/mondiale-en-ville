GLOBAL RULES (every frame, highest priority)
Photoreal live-action film shot in Geneva, Switzerland, on the Quai du Mont-Blanc beside Lake Geneva, warm golden-hour evening light, clean European city, rich cinematic colour, light film grain. One continuous take, no cuts, camera path and timing exactly as Video 1. There is exactly one bus and it never stops, never slows, never changes lane; it speeds along the quay with wheels spinning. The lake is always on the same side of the road as in Video 1; the Haussmann buildings are on the other side. Natural motion blur on anything passing close to the camera. Text appears only in three places and is spelled exactly: the cloud message GENEVA ARE YOU READY?, the bus lettering FROM THE CARIBBEAN TO GENEVA, and the billboard. No other text, shop signs, logos, captions or watermark.

MOMENT 1 (0.0-1.9s)
High above Geneva, among real cumulus clouds, the clouds themselves form the words GENEVA / ARE YOU / READY?, soft, bright and fluffy. The camera flies straight at the cloud words and bursts through them in a swirl of white mist.

MOMENT 2 (1.9-4.0s)
The mist clears and the camera dives toward the city: Lake Geneva with the Jet d'Eau, the tree-lined Quai du Mont-Blanc and rows of grey mansard roofs. A white and orange bus races along the quay far below and grows fast in frame.

MOMENT 3 (4.0-5.4s)
The camera levels out just above the roof of the bus, skimming a roof rack piled with old leather suitcases and trunks. Straight ahead the traffic lights are red, cars wait at the crossing.

MOMENT 4 (5.4-8.0s)
The bus blasts straight through the red light without braking, past the stopped cars. The camera drops to the lake-side flank for a fast side-tracking shot: the white and orange bus fills the frame, FROM THE CARIBBEAN TO GENEVA readable on its band, passengers visible through the open windows, while the cream Haussmann facades, balconies and street lamps whip past behind it in a blur.

MOMENT 5 (8.0-10.6s)
The camera swoops in through an open side window near the front and glides backwards down the centre aisle. Stylish young passengers sit on both sides with old leather suitcases on their laps, laughing, cheering and dancing in their seats, golden light streaming through the windows. The camera flies out through the open rear window.

MOMENT 6 (10.6-12.7s)
Outside behind the bus, the camera swings round over the lakeside promenade to a strikingly beautiful young woman standing at the kerb, talking on her phone, the Jet d'Eau and the Alps behind her. The bus has just roared past her: a gust of wind, dust and autumn leaves whips her long curly hair and her clothes. She lowers the phone and turns to the camera, astonished and amused.

MOMENT 7 (12.7-15.5s)
The camera whips forward and races along the quay, flying over the roof of the bus, toward a giant billboard standing over the road. It pushes in until the billboard fills the whole frame. The billboard is dark with a white border and reads, top to bottom: the yellow Bubble logo, SAT 28 NOV, KULTURE BAR, GENEVA, DJ BLITZ & GOLD UP, 22H - 04H.
